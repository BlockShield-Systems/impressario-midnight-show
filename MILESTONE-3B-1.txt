MILESTONE 3B.1: ENCOUNTER RETRY AND ONE-WAY GATE FLOW

Fixed encounter lifecycle:

- The entrance remains open before the encounter.
- Crossing the arena threshold closes the gate behind the player.
- The player cannot leave while enemies remain alive.
- Death respawns the player at The Main Stage checkpoint.
- Death opens both arena gates.
- All three Stagehands respawn with full health.
- Both Card Throwers respawn with full health.
- All active card projectiles are removed.
- The enemy counter restarts at 0/5.
- Re-entering the arena starts a clean attempt.
- Clearing all five enemies permanently opens the exit.
